## 2. Vendor Service Overview

CloudServe Technologies Ltd. provides a cloud-based customer relationship and workflow management platform.

FinServe Financial Services Ltd. is considering the platform to support customer management, operational workflows, and related business processes.

The proposed service will be delivered as a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) solution, meaning that the vendor will be responsible for the underlying application infrastructure, platform availability, security controls, and technical maintenance associated with the service.

The service may involve the processing and storage of information belonging to or relating to FinServe and its customers.

## 2. Vendor Service Overview

### 2.1 Service Description

CloudServe Technologies Ltd. provides a cloud-based Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and workflow management platform delivered through a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) model.

FinServe Financial Services Ltd. is considering the platform to support customer management, operational workflows, service requests, and related business activities.

The solution is hosted and maintained by CloudServe, with the vendor responsible for the availability, maintenance, underlying infrastructure, and security of the SaaS environment.

### 2.2 Proposed Business Use

FinServe intends to use CloudServe to support:

* Customer relationship management
* Customer service workflows
* Internal operational processes
* Case and service request management
* Business workflow automation
* Reporting and operational visibility

### 2.3 Service Delivery Model

| Attribute                     | Assessment                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Delivery Model**            | Software-as-a-Service (SaaS)                      |
| **Hosting Responsibility**    | Vendor-managed                                    |
| **Infrastructure Management** | Vendor-managed                                    |
| **Application Management**    | Vendor-managed                                    |
| **Customer Access**           | Web-based application                             |
| **Integration**               | API-based integration may be required             |
| **Availability Dependency**   | Dependent on vendor service availability          |
| **Data Storage**              | Vendor-managed cloud environment                  |
| **Support Model**             | Vendor-provided technical and application support |

### 2.4 Business Dependency

The proposed service may become an important component of FinServe's customer management and operational workflows.

If the service becomes unavailable, FinServe may experience disruption to customer service activities, operational processes, and access to information maintained within the platform.

The extent of this dependency will be assessed further during the **Business Criticality and Inherent Risk Assessment**.

### 2.5 Key Risk Considerations

The service model introduces several areas requiring further assessment:

* Reliance on a third-party hosted environment
* Vendor responsibility for application and infrastructure security
* Potential processing of sensitive customer information
* Potential API and system integrations
* Vendor administrative access to the SaaS environment
* Dependence on vendor availability and resilience
* Potential use of subcontractors or cloud infrastructure providers
* Potential cross-border data storage or processing

These considerations will inform the subsequent due diligence and inherent risk assessment.

> **Assessment Note:** The information documented in this section represents the current understanding of the proposed service. Specific technical, data, access, hosting, and subcontractor details will be validated through vendor due diligence and supporting evidence.

## 4. Access, Integration & Connectivity Assessment

### 4.1 Access Overview

The proposed CloudServe service may require access to FinServe users, business information, and potentially integrated systems.

The exact level of access has not yet been validated and will be confirmed through technical due diligence and discussions with the service owner.

The assessment will distinguish between:

* End-user access
* Administrative access
* Privileged access
* API/system-to-system access
* Vendor support access
* Remote access
* Emergency or break-glass access

### 4.2 User Access

FinServe employees are expected to access the CloudServe platform through authenticated user accounts.

The assessment will require confirmation of:

* User authentication mechanisms
* Single Sign-On (SSO) availability
* Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
* Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
* User provisioning and deprovisioning
* Periodic access reviews
* Password and authentication requirements
* Session management
* Inactive account handling

**Current Status:** To be validated during due diligence.

### 4.3 Privileged and Administrative Access

CloudServe personnel may require administrative access to the SaaS environment for platform management, technical support, maintenance, or incident response.

The vendor assessment should establish:

* Whether vendor personnel can access FinServe data
* The number and type of privileged accounts
* Privileged access management controls
* Least-privilege implementation
* MFA requirements for administrative access
* Privileged activity logging and monitoring
* Administrative access review procedures
* Emergency access procedures
* Vendor employee background screening requirements

**Current Status:** To be validated.

### 4.4 System Integration

The proposed service may integrate with FinServe systems through APIs or other approved integration mechanisms.

Potential integration areas may include:

* Identity and access management
* Customer information systems
* Internal workflow platforms
* Reporting systems
* Authentication services
* Security monitoring platforms

The final integration architecture must be reviewed before production implementation.

### 4.5 API and Data Exchange Considerations

If API integration is implemented, the assessment should establish:

* Authentication and authorization mechanisms
* API credential management
* Encryption of data in transit
* API access restrictions
* Rate limiting
* Logging and monitoring
* Token lifecycle management
* Secrets management
* API security testing
* Error handling and information disclosure controls

**Current Status:** Integration requirements are proposed but not yet technically validated.

### 4.6 Network Connectivity

The assessment should determine whether CloudServe requires direct connectivity to FinServe's internal network or whether all communication occurs through controlled internet-based connections.

The preferred architecture should minimize unnecessary network exposure and apply appropriate security controls to any connectivity established between FinServe and CloudServe.

The following should be validated:

* Network architecture
* Connectivity method
* Firewall requirements
* IP allowlisting
* VPN or private connectivity requirements
* Network segmentation
* Inbound and outbound communication paths
* Monitoring of vendor-related traffic

### 4.7 Vendor Support Access

CloudServe support personnel may require temporary access to customer environments or information to troubleshoot incidents or service issues.

The assessment should establish:

* How support access is requested and approved
* Whether access is time-limited
* Whether customer approval is required
* Whether privileged support sessions are monitored
* Whether support activity is logged
* Whether access is automatically revoked
* Whether support personnel are subject to appropriate security requirements

**Current Status:** To be validated.

### 4.8 Access Risk Considerations

Third-party access introduces potential risks including:

* Unauthorized access to FinServe information
* Excessive privileges
* Compromised vendor credentials
* Inadequate authentication controls
* Inadequately monitored administrative activity
* Insecure API integrations
* Uncontrolled remote support access
* Excessive network connectivity
* Failure to revoke access when no longer required

These considerations will contribute to the vendor's **Inherent Risk Assessment** and determine the level of security due diligence required.
