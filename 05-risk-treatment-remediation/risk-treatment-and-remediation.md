# Stage 5 — Risk Treatment & Remediation

## 1. Objective

Define proportionate treatment actions for the findings identified during the Stage 4 control assessment and establish clear ownership, priorities, target timelines, and remediation evidence requirements.

---

## 2. Risk Treatment Approach

The identified findings will primarily be treated through **risk mitigation**.

Because CloudServe is classified as a **Tier 1 — Critical vendor**, remediation priorities are based on potential business impact, security exposure, control weakness, and dependency criticality.

Risk treatment decisions will consider:

- Severity of the finding
- Potential impact to FinServe
- Likelihood of exploitation or occurrence
- Vendor criticality
- Availability of compensating controls
- Required remediation effort
- Evidence required to validate closure

---

---

## 4. Remediation Requirements

### FND-001 — Penetration Testing Findings

**Required evidence:**

- Updated vulnerability/penetration-testing report
- Evidence of remediation
- Validation or retest results
- Documented exceptions where applicable

**Closure condition:** High-severity findings are remediated or formally risk accepted with appropriate compensating controls.

---

### FND-002 — Disaster Recovery RTO

**Required evidence:**

- Corrective action plan
- Updated recovery procedures where applicable
- Recovery test results
- Evidence demonstrating achievement of the required RTO

**Closure condition:** A subsequent recovery exercise demonstrates that the required RTO can be achieved.

---

### FND-003 — Fourth-Party Assurance

**Required evidence:**

- Current security assessment or independent assurance report
- Updated fourth-party risk information
- Evidence of review by the responsible vendor-risk function

**Closure condition:** Current assurance evidence is obtained and reviewed, with material risks incorporated into ongoing oversight.

---

### FND-004 — Data Protection Incident Notification

**Required evidence:**

- Updated Data Processing Agreement
- Defined notification timelines
- Documented responsibilities and escalation requirements

**Closure condition:** Contractual requirements are formally clarified and aligned with the organization's incident-response and regulatory obligations.

---

## 5. Remediation Governance

Remediation will be monitored through the vendor risk management process.

For each finding:

1. The remediation action is assigned to an accountable owner.
2. A target completion date is established.
3. Progress is tracked against the agreed timeline.
4. Supporting evidence is collected.
5. Remediation effectiveness is validated.
6. Overdue or materially unresolved findings are escalated.
7. Closure is approved by the appropriate risk owner.

High-severity findings receive increased monitoring and escalation compared with Medium-severity findings.
