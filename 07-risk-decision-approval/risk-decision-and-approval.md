# Stage 7 — Risk Decision & Approval

## 1. Objective

Determine whether the organization should proceed with the vendor relationship based on the residual risk identified in Stage 6 and establish the conditions required for continued engagement.

---

## 2. Decision Basis

The decision considers:

- Vendor criticality
- Residual risk level
- Outstanding remediation requirements
- Control effectiveness
- Business impact
- Availability of compensating controls
- Risk appetite and tolerance
- Required management oversight

CloudServe Technologies Ltd. remains classified as a **Tier 1 — Critical** vendor. The residual-risk assessment identified an overall **Medium residual risk**, with no High residual risks remaining following the proposed treatment measures.

---

## 3. Risk Decision

### Recommended Decision

**Conditional Approval — Proceed with Remediation Requirements**

The vendor relationship may proceed subject to completion and validation of the remediation actions identified in Stage 5.

This decision recognizes that the vendor provides a critical service and that terminating or delaying the relationship solely because of the identified findings may not be proportionate where appropriate remediation and monitoring can reduce the exposure to an acceptable level.

---

## 4. Conditions of Approval

The following conditions apply:

1. High-severity findings must remain subject to formal remediation tracking.
2. Required remediation evidence must be provided and independently reviewed where appropriate.
3. Disaster recovery remediation must be validated through subsequent testing.
4. Current assurance evidence for the critical fourth party must be obtained.
5. Data-protection incident notification requirements must be formally clarified.
6. Material changes to the vendor's service, security posture, ownership, or critical dependencies must trigger reassessment.
7. The vendor must remain subject to enhanced ongoing monitoring due to its Tier 1 classification.

Failure to meet material remediation requirements may result in escalation, additional risk treatment, formal risk acceptance, suspension of the relationship, or termination consideration.

---

| Decision Element | Responsibility |

The final decision should be recorded and approved by an individual with appropriate authority under the organization's risk governance framework.

> **Case Study Note:** Specific organizational names and approval authorities are illustrative for this portfolio case study.

---

## 6. Escalation Criteria

The vendor relationship should be escalated for further management review if:

- High-severity findings remain unresolved beyond agreed deadlines.
- Remediation evidence cannot be validated.
- Recovery testing continues to fail required objectives.
- Material fourth-party risks remain unidentified or unmanaged.
- Contractual privacy requirements remain unresolved.
- The vendor experiences a material security incident.
- The vendor's risk profile materially increases.
- Residual risk exceeds the organization's approved risk appetite.

## 8. Stage 7 Outcome

**Decision:** Conditional Approval — Proceed with Remediation Requirements

The organization may continue the vendor relationship while the outstanding remediation actions are tracked and validated.

Because CloudServe is a Tier 1 — Critical vendor, approval does not end the risk-management process. The relationship will transition into **Stage 8 — Ongoing Monitoring & Reporting**, where changes in risk posture, control performance, remediation status, incidents, and third-party dependencies will be continuously monitored.
