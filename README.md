# Third-Party Risk Management Case Study

## Overview

A practical, risk-based Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) case study demonstrating how an organization can assess, treat, approve, and continuously monitor a critical technology vendor.

The case study follows a complete vendor risk lifecycle using a simulated vendor scenario and clearly defined GRC artifacts.

> **Portfolio Disclaimer:** This is a simulated case study created for professional portfolio and learning purposes. Vendor information, evidence, findings, risk ratings, and assessment results are illustrative.

---

## Project Objective

The objective of this case study is to demonstrate a structured approach to third-party risk management, including:

- Vendor profiling and business assessment
- Inherent risk assessment and vendor tiering
- Risk-based due diligence
- Evidence collection and validation
- Control assessment
- Findings identification and remediation
- Residual risk assessment
- Risk-based decision-making
- Ongoing vendor monitoring

The approach emphasizes **risk proportionality, evidence-based assessment, and continuous oversight** rather than treating vendor assessment as a one-time compliance exercise.

---

## Vendor Scenario

**Vendor:** CloudServe Technologies Ltd.

**Service:** Cloud infrastructure and managed technology services

**Vendor Tier:** Tier 1 — Critical

**Inherent Risk:** 4.20 / 5 — Critical

**Residual Risk:** Medium

**Final Decision:** Conditional Approval

**Monitoring Level:** Enhanced

The vendor is treated as critical because of its business dependency, access to sensitive information, technology integration, and potential impact on business operations.
