# Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) Case Study

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end, risk-based Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) assessment for a fictional financial services organization and its critical SaaS vendor.

The case study is designed to demonstrate how a GRC professional can identify, assess, manage, and monitor third-party risks throughout the vendor lifecycle.

The assessment covers the process from initial vendor intake through inherent risk assessment, due diligence, control evaluation, risk treatment, residual risk assessment, and ongoing monitoring.

> **Disclaimer:** CloudServe Technologies Ltd. and FinServe Financial Services Ltd. are fictional organizations created solely for this portfolio case study. No confidential or proprietary organizational information is used.

---

## Business Scenario

### Client Organization

**FinServe Financial Services Ltd.**

FinServe is a fictional financial services organization that relies on technology-enabled services to support customer-facing and internal business operations.

### Third-Party Vendor

**CloudServe Technologies Ltd.**

CloudServe is a fictional Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) provider offering a cloud-based customer relationship and workflow management platform.

The platform is being considered for use by FinServe to support customer management, operational workflows, and related business processes.

Because the proposed service may involve sensitive information, system integrations, administrative access, and significant business dependency, FinServe requires a structured third-party risk assessment before approving the engagement.

---

## Project Objectives

The objectives of this assessment are to:

* Establish the vendor's business and service profile.
* Determine the vendor's inherent risk and criticality.
* Define appropriate due diligence requirements based on risk.
* Evaluate security and compliance evidence provided by the vendor.
* Assess relevant controls against recognized security frameworks.
* Identify and document third-party risks and control gaps.
* Develop appropriate risk treatment and remediation actions.
* Determine residual risk following treatment.
* Provide a recommendation regarding the vendor relationship.
* Establish an appropriate ongoing monitoring approach.

---

## TPRM Lifecycle

The project follows the lifecycle below:

**Vendor Intake → Business Assessment → Inherent Risk → Vendor Tiering → Due Diligence → Evidence & Control Assessment → Risk Register → Risk Treatment → Residual Risk → Final Recommendation → Ongoing Monitoring**
