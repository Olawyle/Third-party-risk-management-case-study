## 2. Purpose

This questionnaire is designed to assess the security, privacy, resilience, and governance controls of CloudServe Technologies Ltd.

The questionnaire has been intentionally scoped according to the vendor's inherent risk profile, business criticality, data exposure, access requirements, and fourth-party dependencies.

The objective is not to collect the maximum amount of information from the vendor, but to obtain sufficient evidence to determine whether the vendor's controls adequately address the risks identified during the inherent risk assessment.

## 3. Response Classification

Vendor responses will be classified as follows:

| Status | Definition |

| **Accepted** | Response and evidence are considered sufficient |

| **Not Applicable** | Requirement does not apply, subject to justification |

# 15. Evidence-Based Assessment Approach

Vendor responses will not automatically be treated as evidence of control effectiveness.

Where a response indicates that a control exists, supporting evidence should be requested where appropriate.

Examples include:

| Vendor Statement | Supporting Evidence |
|---|---|
| "We use MFA." | IAM policy/configuration evidence |
| "We perform penetration testing." | Penetration test executive summary |
| "We are ISO 27001 certified." | Current certificate and relevant scope |
| "We have disaster recovery." | DR test summary |
| "We monitor security events." | Monitoring/logging documentation |
| "We manage subprocessors." | Subprocessor inventory and governance procedure |
