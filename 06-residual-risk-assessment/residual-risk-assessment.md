# Stage 6 — Residual Risk Assessment

## 1. Objective

Assess the level of risk remaining after considering the vendor's existing controls and the treatment actions defined in Stage 5.

The purpose is to determine whether the remaining exposure is within the organization's acceptable risk tolerance or requires additional treatment or formal risk acceptance.

---

## 2. Residual Risk Approach

Residual risk was assessed by considering:

- Existing control effectiveness
- Severity of identified findings
- Planned remediation actions
- Potential business impact
- Likelihood of the risk occurring
- Criticality of the vendor
- Remaining control gaps
- Availability of compensating controls

Residual risk reflects the exposure that remains after the current control environment and planned treatment measures are considered.

> **Assessment Note:** This is a simulated portfolio case study. Residual-risk ratings and treatment assumptions are illustrative and would require validation against the organization's actual risk appetite and approved methodology in a real engagement.

## 4. Residual Risk Analysis

### FND-001 — Penetration Testing Findings

Residual risk remains **Medium** because remediation reduces the immediate exposure, but the vendor's criticality and dependency on externally exposed systems mean ongoing vulnerability management and validation remain necessary.

**Required monitoring:** Vulnerability remediation, penetration testing, and validation evidence.

---

### FND-002 — Disaster Recovery RTO

Residual risk remains **Medium** because successful remediation depends on demonstrating that the required recovery objective can be consistently achieved.

**Required monitoring:** Recovery testing results, RTO performance, and corrective-action status.

---

### FND-003 — Fourth-Party Assurance

Residual risk reduces to **Low** once current assurance evidence is obtained and reviewed. Continued oversight remains necessary because the fourth party represents a critical dependency within the vendor ecosystem.

**Required monitoring:** Fourth-party assurance status and reassessment dates.

---

### FND-004 — Data Protection Incident Notification

Residual risk reduces to **Low** once notification requirements are formally documented and incorporated into the contractual and incident-response framework.

**Required monitoring:** Contractual compliance and periodic review of data-protection requirements.

---

## 5. Overall Residual Risk

The assessment indicates that remediation reduces the identified exposure from the original inherent-risk position.

**Overall Residual Risk Rating: Medium**

The vendor remains a **Tier 1 — Critical** relationship. Although the planned treatment reduces the identified control weaknesses, the critical nature of the service means residual risk cannot be considered negligible.

The remaining Medium risks should therefore remain subject to management oversight and remediation validation.

---

## 6. Risk Acceptance Consideration

At this stage, no formal risk acceptance is recorded.

The remaining Medium risks should be reviewed by the appropriate risk owner after remediation evidence has been validated.

If the residual risk remains above the organization's approved risk appetite, additional treatment or formal risk acceptance should be considered.
